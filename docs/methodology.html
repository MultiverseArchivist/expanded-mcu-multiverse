Methodology

Which entries count

Main includes core MCU narratives, directly connected multiverse stories, selected legacy titles, and original in-universe promotions. Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. follows this project’s canon rule. Optional & Excluded includes selected supplemental stories and titles with qualified continuity. Documentaries & Extras covers nonfiction and recaps. Upcoming and aggregate projects sit outside the marathon totals.

Release order

Place entries by original public release date, generally in the U.S. Historical dates and cuts can differ by source. Supplemental reminders cover the period after the current Main release date through the next Main release date, including that next date.

Evidence and access

Catalogs, publisher listings, archives, and databases inform dates, runtimes, and scope. Planning minutes may use a published runtime or an estimate. A catalog listing does not establish playback or reading entitlement. Historical references can remain useful when an authorized route is unavailable.

Permanent IDs

IDs identify the same record when its title, date, classification, or worksheet changes. Announced projects can keep an ID through release, and individual episodes receive distinct IDs from their aggregate parent. Prefixes record the original assignment.
