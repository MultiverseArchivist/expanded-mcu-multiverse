Spreadsheet compatibility

Development transparency

The Expanded MCU Multiverse Tracker is independently maintained and developed with assistance from AI tools, including OpenAI’s ChatGPT. AI assisted with research, spreadsheet development, automation, documentation, and quality assurance. The maintainer directs project scope, inclusion criteria, editorial decisions, and release approval.

Research is checked against published sources where possible. The workbook flags uncertainty, and community corrections are welcome.
