# Expanded MCU Multiverse

**Community Edition v3.0.0** · Maintained by **The Multiverse Archivist**

[Project website](https://multiversearchivist.github.io/expanded-mcu-multiverse/) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/MultiverseArchivist/expanded-mcu-multiverse) · [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MultiverseArchivist/expanded-mcu-multiverse/issues)

*A release-order marathon tracker for anyone looking to rediscover everything connected to the Marvel Cinematic Universe.*

Free to download. Free to use. Built by a Marvel fan, for Marvel fans.

## How It All Started

On Thursday, November 12, 2026, I realized there was some newer Marvel content that I hadn't watched yet. To my surprise, I saw that Disney+ had announced a new cut, [*Avengers Endgame: Encore*](https://press.disney.co.uk/news/marvel-studios-avengers-endgame-encore-debuts-friday-on-disney-plus), for the next day. So you know I had to watch it.

I hadn't seen *Endgame* since it first released back in 2019, so it was pretty exciting to experience it all over again. But it left me wanting more.

While I had missed some of the newer entries, I was almost certain I had seen everything else since 2008's *Iron Man*. So I decided to do what I had done before *Endgame* came out: embark on another MCU marathon.

Everywhere I looked, watch lists seemed to favor chronological order, but where's the fun in that? I wanted to start from the beginning and work my way through to the newest stuff.

Well, long story short, *Deadpool & Wolverine* opened up the multiverse even further. Suddenly, my MCU marathon was becoming something much bigger.

Before this expedition, I never realized just how many comics directly tied into the movies. In working with ChatGPT to assemble this new list, I continued to uncover more and more content connected, sometimes loosely, to the main MCU timeline.

What started as a personal checklist quickly evolved into a comprehensive marathon tracker.

Two weeks later, I've decided that I have a really great working product, and it deserves to be shared.

So buckle in for over 550 hours of content if you want to rediscover the MCU with me!

## What Counts as Canon?

I know there will always be debates over what is and what isn't canon. In assembling this list, I've relied on officially confirmed sources and a little bit of my own intuition.

Take *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, for example. To me, it's quite obvious from the beginning that Season 1 is connected to the main MCU timeline. Whether or not they stay on that timeline throughout the series is another debate entirely. As far as I'm concerned, that's where their story originated, and therefore it deserves its place in this marathon.

I've applied similar reasoning to other Marvel productions. If there's a meaningful connection to the MCU, whether established elsewhere in the MCU or within the production itself, I believe it's worth exploring.

The purpose of this list isn't to establish an official canon or settle every continuity debate.

**This list isn't meant to cover Earth-616 alone. It's meant to explore anything and everything connected to its universe.**

You may disagree with some of my decisions, and that's perfectly fine. That's part of the fun of being a Marvel fan.

## Why Release Order?

I think the best part of watching in release order is realizing just how much you've forgotten since you last saw these stories. Even if you've watched every MCU movie, there's a good chance you'll rediscover moments, characters and connections that you had completely forgotten about.

The next best part is seeing how Marvel's storytelling has developed over nearly two decades. From standalone superhero movies to interconnected television shows and eventually an entire multiverse, it's incredible to look back at how far Marvel has come.

For me, my MCU journey began when *Iron Man* first came out in 2008, and I've been hooked ever since. Going back through these older entries serves as a throwback to those days, as well as a reminder of why I kept watching in the first place.

Along the way, you might even discover something you never knew existed. I certainly have.

There's nothing wrong with watching in chronological order, but for this marathon, I want to rediscover Marvel as it unfolded.

## Why Share It?

One of my goals when building this tracker was to create something I could come back to another ten years from now. Something I could keep up to date with the latest and greatest Marvel content, ready for whenever I decide to embark on another marathon.

When I got to the point of debating whether or not to share it with the public, the answer was a resounding "Do it!"

Surely I'm not the only person looking for an "everything there is to the MCU" guide.

Whether you're a longtime Marvel fan looking to rediscover these stories, someone who wants to experience the MCU for the first time, or someone interested in uncovering content you never knew existed, I hope this project gives you a place to start.

I intend to keep this tracker updated as Marvel continues to expand its universe. And thanks to the upgrade utility, you won't have to start your marathon over every time a new version is released.

This started as my personal marathon. Now, I'd like to share it with anyone who wants to come along.

### Understanding the Categories

**Main:** Stories included in the primary marathon based on the project's continuity and multiverse inclusion criteria.

**Optional & Excluded:** Supplemental stories, less definitive connections and selected productions that are documented separately from the primary marathon.

**Documentaries & Extras:** Behind-the-scenes material, nonfiction productions, recaps and other supplemental releases.

**Future Releases:** Announced upcoming projects and their known release information.

Optional content, documentaries and unreleased projects do not count toward completion of the Main marathon.

## Getting Started

1. Download the latest Community Edition from the [project website](https://multiversearchivist.github.io/expanded-mcu-multiverse/).
2. Extract the distribution ZIP if necessary.
3. Save a working copy of the workbook.
4. Open the Getting Started worksheet for navigation instructions.
5. Visit the Marathon Hub to find your next unfinished Main entry.
6. Update your progress in the Canon Release Checklist as you work through the marathon.

On the Main checklist:

- Column H records your viewing or reading status.
- Column I records your completion date using YYYY-MM-DD.
- Column J contains the official planning estimate in minutes.
- Column Q allows you to enter an optional personal rating from 1 to 10.

Follow the supplemental content reminders to discover Optional and Documentary releases appearing between Main entries.

Make the marathon your own. You don't have to watch or read everything to enjoy the experience.

## Updating Your Workbook

One of my priorities was making sure that people wouldn't lose their progress whenever I released an updated tracker.

The project includes a browser-based upgrade utility that transfers supported personal progress into a newer Community Edition.

1. Open the workbook updater on the project website.
2. Select your existing Community Edition workbook.
3. Allow the updater to retrieve the latest release, or select a new workbook manually.
4. Run the migration.
5. Review the migration summary and any flagged conflicts.
6. Download your updated workbook.
7. Open it and verify your progress before replacing your previous copy.

Your workbook is processed locally within your browser. It is not uploaded to a project server or GitHub for processing.

The updater preserves your original input files and generates a separate upgraded workbook.

An offline Python migration utility is also included for users who prefer that approach. See the [offline upgrade guide](docs/offline/UPGRADE.md) for instructions.

Supported personal progress includes completion statuses, dates and ratings. Official inventory information comes from the newer workbook. Custom changes to planning estimates and official source links may require manual review.

Always retain a backup of your existing workbook until you have verified the upgraded copy.

## Compatibility

The tracker is distributed as a macro-free XLSX workbook.

The workbook and migration utilities have been tested using LibreOffice Calc. The browser updater has also been successfully tested in Firefox and Microsoft Edge, with migrated workbooks opened and recalculated in desktop Microsoft Excel.

Comprehensive compatibility with every Excel version and spreadsheet application has not been established.

For the best experience, I recommend using a desktop or laptop. The project website is mobile-friendly, but mobile spreadsheet applications may have limitations involving workbook layouts, navigation and functionality.

## Finding the Content

Where possible, the tracker includes links to streaming providers, publishers, retailers and other relevant sources.

Availability can vary by region, subscription, purchase requirements and edition.

Some older promotional videos, comics and supplemental releases may no longer have a confirmed authorized viewing or reading source.

An entry appearing in the tracker does not guarantee that it is currently available.

The project does not distribute Marvel films, television episodes, comics or other copyrighted media.

Published release dates and runtimes may also vary between sources. I encourage corrections supported by reliable evidence.
